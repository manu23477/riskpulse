# P2.6 SPATIAL & ADMINISTRATIVE INTEGRATION

- **Spatial Integration**: References `spatialStateId` and `spatialStateVersion` from P2.4 `SpatialState`.
- **Administrative Integration**: References `administrativeStateId` and `administrativeStateVersion` from P2.5 `AdministrativeState`.
