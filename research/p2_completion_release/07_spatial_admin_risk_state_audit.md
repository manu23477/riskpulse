# P2 SPATIAL, ADMINISTRATIVE, AND RISK STATE AUDIT

- **SpatialState**: Preserves `EPSG:4326` WGS84 spatial representation, CRS, and location vs footprint extent distinction.
- **AdministrativeState**: Preserves Revenue ($\text{District} \rightarrow \text{Tehsil} \rightarrow \text{Village}$) and Development ($\text{District} \rightarrow \text{Block} \rightarrow \text{GP} \rightarrow \text{Village}$) parallel chains.
- **DynamicRiskState**: Captures `hazardCondition`, `exposureCondition`, `vulnerabilityCondition`, `impactCondition`, and `confidenceScore`.
