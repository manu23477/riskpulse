## 1. TECHNICAL COMBINATION EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

To evaluate potential multi-reference combination risks without making legal obviousness conclusions, each combination is audited across 6 objective technical dimensions:
1. **Component Coverage**: Which K-components ($K01..K10$) are covered by the combination?
2. **Relationship Coverage**: Which technical relationships ($R01..R30$) are formed?
3. **Missing Relationships**: Which critical coupling relationships remain absent?
4. **Technical Motivation**: Is there an explicit teaching or motivation to combine the references?
5. **Architectural Compatibility**: Are the data models, state transition mechanisms, and spatial coordinate systems compatible?
6. **Hindsight Dependence**: Is the combination apparent only after knowing RiskPulse's architecture?
