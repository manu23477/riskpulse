## 1. THE 18-MONTH PATENT PUBLICATION BLIND SPOT

Under international patent laws (35 U.S.C. § 122(b), PCT Article 21, India Patent Act Section 11A):
1. **Statutory 18-Month Rule**: Patent applications are maintained in statutory secrecy and published approximately **18 months after their earliest filing or priority date**.
2. **Current Blind Spot**: Patent applications filed between **April 1, 2025 and October 1, 2026** remain inherently unsearchable in public databases.
3. **Nonpublication Requests**: In the US (35 U.S.C. § 122(b)(2)(B)), applicants filing solely in the US may request nonpublication until grant.
4. **Research Limitation**: This prior-art search cannot and does not claim to cover unpublished applications, trade secrets, or unfiled internal R&D.
