# RISKPULSE PATENT WINDOW 1: QUESTIONS FOR PATENT COUNSEL

**Document Identifier**: `PW1_COUNSEL_QUESTIONS`  
**Purpose**: High-quality structured questions for qualified patent counsel regarding Patent Window 1 (Evidence Fusion) draft claims and prior-art positioning.

---

### Key Questions for Legal Counsel:

1. **Candidate Subject Matter**: Which technical combination (Combination C07, Combination C08, or an integrated joint combination) represents the strongest primary subject matter for initial patent drafting?
2. **Filing Strategy**: Should Combination C07 (multi-event shared administrative risk DAG propagation) and Combination C08 (bitemporal late-evidence state revision) be pursued as separate independent claims within a single application, or as distinct divisional/continuation applications?
3. **Disclosed vs Combination Boundaries**: Given that generic software DAG invalidation (`US7441230B2`), selective recomputation (`EP3622411B1`), and bitemporal stream ingestion (`US20200379978A1`) are disclosed in prior art, does the specific coupling to multi-layer spatial administrative risk crosswalks overcome 35 U.S.C. § 103 (obviousness) rejections?
4. **Technical Character**: Does cross-event isolation (preserving Event B hypothesis state $100\%$ unpoisoned while updating shared Administrative Unit X) provide sufficient "technical effect" under EPO Guidelines (G-II, 3.6) and USPTO Alice Step 2A/2B guidelines for software-implemented GIS methods?
5. **Non-Destructive Fusion**: Does non-deletion contradiction retention in evidence lineage graphs contribute to patentable subject matter or is it likely to be characterized as abstract data representation?
6. **Bitemporal Revision**: Is the separation of event occurrence time ($t_{\text{event}}$) from processing arrival time ($t_{\text{arrival}}$) in multi-layer GIS risk graphs sufficiently distinguished from standard database logging prior art (`US20200379978A1`)?
7. **Claim Framing**: Should the primary independent claims be framed around:
   - A computer-implemented method?
   - A disaster intelligence processing system?
   - A non-transitory computer-readable storage medium?
   - A combination of all three?
8. **Claim Granularity**: Which level of claim granularity (Broad, Medium-Granularity, or Narrow Mechanism) offers the optimal balance between claim scope and prior-art defensibility?
9. **Additional Prior-Art Searching**: What additional specialized international Patent Classification (IPC/CPC) subclasses (e.g., G08B, G06Q50/26, G06F16/24, G06F16/29) or non-patent literature repositories (IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library) should be searched prior to formal filing?
10. **Enablement & Best Mode**: Does the experimental research harness documentation (`PW1C-1B` and `PW1C-3`) satisfy 35 U.S.C. § 112 enablement requirements without disclosing proprietary production algorithms?
11. **Provisional Filing**: Should a U.S. Provisional Patent Application be filed immediately based on this dossier before public research publication?
12. **PCT International Search**: Would an international filing under the Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT) be advantageous for establishing an early international priority date across multiple jurisdictions (USPTO, EPO, WIPO)?
13. **Subject Matter Eligibility**: How should the claims be drafted to avoid potential 35 U.S.C. § 101 rejections concerning abstract mental processes or mathematical concepts?
14. **Cross-Domain Application**: Should claim scope be restricted specifically to disaster risk intelligence, or broadened to dynamic geospatial entity graph processing across transportation, supply-chain, and infrastructure networks?
15. **Freedom-to-Operate**: What specific steps should be taken to perform a formal Freedom-to-Operate (FTO) investigation prior to commercial deployment of the selective propagation engine?
