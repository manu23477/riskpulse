## 2. DISAMBIGUATION OF PUBLISHED SOURCES

1. **Revenue Geography**: Survey of India, LGD, and HP Revenue Department publish and maintain the Revenue Hierarchy (State $\rightarrow$ District $\rightarrow$ Sub-Division $\rightarrow$ Tehsil / Sub-Tehsil $\rightarrow$ Revenue Village).
2. **Development Geography**: HP Department of Rural Development and Panchayati Raj publishes and maintains the Development Hierarchy (State $\rightarrow$ District $\rightarrow$ Development Block $\rightarrow$ Gram Panchayat).
3. **Data Isolation Invariant**: The two hierarchies operate in parallel and do NOT share identical sub-district boundaries.
