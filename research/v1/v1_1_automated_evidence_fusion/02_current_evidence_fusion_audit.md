# V1.1 CURRENT EVIDENCE FUSION AUDIT

Audited pre-existing evidence handling:
- `EvidenceObject` holds source system, publisher, and content hash.
- P2.1 `NegativeEvidence` handles analytical contradictions without erasing events.
- P2.2 `EventHypothesisRevisionService` executes versioned hypothesis updates.
- V1.1 `EvidenceFusionService` orchestrates these layers into an automated, explainable multi-source fusion assessment.
