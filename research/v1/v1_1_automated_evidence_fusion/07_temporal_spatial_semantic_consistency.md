# V1.1 TEMPORAL, SPATIAL & SEMANTIC CONSISTENCY

- **Spatial Consistency**: Evaluates observation geometry vs target event footprint (`CONSISTENT`, `OVERLAPPING`, `NEARBY`, `INCONSISTENT`).
- **Temporal Consistency**: Compares observation timestamps vs target event window (`CONSISTENT`, `TEMPORALLY_ADJACENT`, `INCONSISTENT`).
- **Semantic Compatibility**: Classifies evidence as `CORROBORATING`, `DIMENSION_CONTRADICTION`, or `NEUTRAL`.
